Privacy Policy

Last updated August 5, 2026

Boardcall is a surf-briefing app. This page explains what data the app touches and, just as important, what it doesn't.

No account required

Boardcall works fully without creating an account, signing in, or providing an email address. Your surfer profile, your boards, and your favorite spots are stored only on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your profile (skill level, weight, height)
  • Your boards (dimensions, type, fin setup)
  • Your favorite spots
  • How many AI-written briefings you've used this week

None of this leaves your phone except as described below.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Boardcall offers an optional Sign in with Apple button. It doesn't unlock anything today — it exists so a future sync feature won't require signing in twice. If you use it, Apple shares your name and a unique identifier with the app. That information is stored on your device only and is never sent to us, to a server, or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time from Settings › Account › Sign Out.

Daily briefings

When you open Today or Forecast, Boardcall sends the surf conditions and your ranked boards — no name, no account, no precise location — to our server, which asks Anthropic's Claude API to write a plain-English briefing. That request is not stored after the response is generated. An anonymous, randomly generated device identifier is used only to apply a fair-use limit on these requests; it identifies a device, not a person.

Location

Boardcall does not use location services. Spots are chosen from a fixed, built-in list — the app never reads your device's location.

Subscriptions

Boardcall Pro purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details.

Third-party weather data

Wave and wind forecasts come from Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Tide predictions come from NOAA CO-OPS. Both are public, unauthenticated APIs — the only information sent to them is the coordinates of a fixed spot from our built-in list, never anything about you.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad trackers
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties
  • No data collected from children — Boardcall isn't directed at children under 13

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new date at the top.
