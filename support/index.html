Frequently asked questions

Q. Why does my briefing read differently some mornings — is it not using AI?

Every briefing is normally written by an AI service, using your day's conditions and boards. If that call fails for any reason — no connection, a server hiccup, or you've used up your free AI briefings for the week — Boardcall instantly writes a solid briefing itself instead, from the same underlying numbers and the same ranked list of your boards. You'll never see an error screen either way.

AI-written briefings carry a small sparkle mark and a "Written by AI" label, so you can always tell which kind you're reading. Both are built from the same forecast data and the same on-device board ranking; the difference is that one is written from a template and the other is written by Claude, which may also explain a different pick from that ranked list. AI-written text can be wrong — check anything that matters against the water in front of you.

Q. Why did my board pick change from yesterday?

The pick is based on today's actual conditions — wave size, period, and wind — measured against the volume and type of every board in your quiver. Nothing about your boards changed; the surf did, so a different one can be the better fit today.

Q. Does Boardcall work without an internet connection?

Boardcall needs a connection to fetch the day's wave, wind, and tide data. If you've opened the app recently, it'll show your last cached conditions (cached for up to an hour). Without a connection and without anything cached yet, you'll need to get online to see today's briefing.

Q. How do I change my spot or notification time, or turn notifications off?

Open the Me tab. Your break and your daily reminder time both live there, and you can also switch breaks with Change at the top of Today. To stop the daily reminder, turn off its Notification switch in Me — you can also turn off notifications for Boardcall entirely in iOS Settings → Notifications → Boardcall. (Before version 1.0.14, these are in Settings, behind the gear icon on Today.)

Q. How do I add, edit, or delete a board?

Open the Me tab. Tap Add a board to add one, tap an existing board to edit its dimensions, type, volume, or fin setup, or swipe left on it to delete it. (Before version 1.0.14, boards are in the Quiver tab.)

Q. What does Boardcall Pro unlock, and what does it cost?

The free version is the daily call at your break: today's and tomorrow's call with the board to bring, your full board quiver with no limits, the map with every break's call, the widget, the daily reminder, and the Sessions tab with a score for every session you log, including sessions imported from Apple Health. You save one break at a time and can switch it to any break in the catalog whenever you like. Today always shows a complete, accurate briefing; on the free version it's written on your phone rather than by AI, as described above.

Boardcall Pro is for planning the week. It adds the rest of the seven-day forecast, up to three saved breaks, the best break near yours right now, and good-day alerts: a heads-up the evening before a day that looks good at one of your breaks. It adds an AI-written briefing every day, all your breaks across the week at a glance and the week at any break on the map, the best window across the week, a coaching note at most once a week on patterns in your own logged sessions, where your quiver comes up short for your break, extra daily reminders beyond the one you already set, side-by-side comparison of two of your boards against today's conditions, and your session trends and records, with heart-rate zones and, where your watch saved a route, waves, top speed and a map for each imported session.

If you started using Boardcall before version 1.0.14, you keep the full seven-day forecast, up to three saved breaks and the best break nearby for free. If you started before version 1.0.15, you keep 4 AI-written briefings a week for free. Good-day alerts are new in 1.0.14, so they are part of Pro for everyone.

Pro doesn't remove the daily limit that protects the AI service from abuse. If that limit is reached, or the AI service is unavailable, Today shows the locally written briefing instead.

Q. Is there a free trial?

When one is on offer, it is on the annual plan: 7 days free, for Apple Accounts that haven't had an introductory offer for Boardcall Pro before. The app shows the trial on its purchase screen whenever it is available. The monthly plan has no trial. Apple allows one introductory offer per Apple Account for Boardcall Pro, across both plans, so an account that has already had one is offered the subscription without a trial. Apple's purchase sheet shows whether a trial applies and when it ends before you confirm.

Nothing is charged when you start the trial. Unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the annual price (or the monthly price, if you switched to the monthly plan during the trial) is charged to your Apple Account within the 24 hours before the trial ends, and the subscription then renews automatically. To avoid being charged, turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in either of the places described below. If you do, nothing is charged and Pro stays on until the trial ends. Go by the end date Apple shows you when you start the trial.

Q. How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Me tab and, under Subscription, tap Restore Purchases (before version 1.0.14: Settings → Subscription). Since Apple handles all billing, this reconnects Boardcall to your existing subscription under the Apple Account you purchased with — Boardcall itself keeps no purchase records of its own.

Q. How do I cancel my subscription?

Billing for Boardcall Pro is handled by Apple, and you can cancel in either of two places. In the app, open the Me tab (before version 1.0.14, Settings) and, under Subscription, tap Manage or cancel subscription, which opens Apple's subscription screen. Or, in iOS, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Boardcall Pro → Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It doesn't end the current period, and Pro stays on until that period, or your free trial, runs out. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period or trial ends.

Q. Does Boardcall use my location?

No. Boardcall never asks for location access. Every break in the app — on Today, the week's forecast, or the map — comes from a built-in catalog you choose from, and "near you" means near the breaks you saved. The one optional exception: if you import sessions from Apple Health and turn on Include GPS, Boardcall reads the route your watch recorded for that surf, on your phone only, to count waves and draw the session.

Q. How do I import sessions from Apple Health?

In the Me tab, turn on Import surf sessions from Apple Health and allow the workout data iOS asks about. After you surf with a watch that records a surfing workout to Apple Health, Today offers to log it with the time already filled in; you pick the board and a rating. Nothing is logged until you tap Save, and Health data never leaves your phone. To stop it, turn the switch off, or remove Boardcall's access in the iPhone's Settings app under Health, Data Access & Devices.
