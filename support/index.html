Common questions

Why does today's briefing look generic or say the forecast is unavailable?
If our briefing service is unreachable, Boardcall automatically falls back to a locally written briefing built from the same forecast data — you'll never see a blank screen or an error. Pull down to refresh once you're back online for the full AI-written version.

How do I add or edit a board?
Open the Quiver tab and tap the + button. You can search a built-in catalog of production boards to prefill dimensions, or enter your own — every value stays editable afterward.

How do I change my home spot?
Settings › Your Breaks. You can favorite up to three spots; tap the star to set which one drives Today and Forecast.

What does Boardcall Pro unlock?
One thing: it removes the weekly limit on Claude-written briefings. Everything else — your full quiver, the map, and every spot in the catalog — is free, with no cap, whether or not you subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Boardcall.
