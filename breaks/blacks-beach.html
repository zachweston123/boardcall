Break guide · San Diego, CA

Black's Beach

A beach break in San Diego, CA. Here is when it tends to be good, and what to know before you go.

When it works

Black's Beach picks up swell coming from the south round to the northwest. It's cleanest when the wind blows from the east, which is offshore here and smooths the waves.

The wave

A beach break. Waves break over sandbars, which shift with the season.

Getting there and crowds

Getting down is the real crux — the main Torrey Pines Gliderport trail drops roughly 300 feet down a steep, genuinely unstable cliff that the city has posted a do-not-use warning on, so plenty of regulars instead walk in from the north or south at low tide. That access hassle keeps the lineup thinner than the wave quality would suggest, though it fills in fast once a good swell gets around. Watch for rip currents that commonly set up off the north and south peaks, plus loose rock and slide debris near the base of the bluffs.
