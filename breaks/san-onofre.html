San Onofre (Old Man's)

A point break in San Clemente, CA. Here is when it tends to be good, and what to know before you go.

When it works

San Onofre (Old Man's) picks up swell coming from the south round to the west. It's cleanest when the wind blows from the northeast, which is offshore here and smooths the waves.

The wave

A point break. Waves wrap around a headland and peel along it.

Getting there and crowds

This is inside San Onofre State Beach, so you need a day-use parking permit ($15 weekday, $20 summer weekends) at the gate; from there you drive down a short hill onto a hard-packed dirt road that runs right along the sand, so you park a few steps from the water. It's the classic mellow, beginner- and longboard-friendly wave with a decades-deep surf culture, but that reputation means it gets crowded — the beachfront parking is often full by 8am on weekends.
