Doheny State Beach

A beach break in Dana Point, CA. Here is when it tends to be good, and what to know before you go.

When it works

Doheny State Beach picks up swell coming from the south round to the west. It's cleanest when the wind blows from the northeast, which is offshore here and smooths the waves.

The wave

A beach break. Waves break over sandbars, which shift with the season.

Getting there and crowds

Two day-use lots: the north lot sits closest to the surf and the harbor, the south lot is narrower with direct beach access and fire rings. Don't wade across the San Juan Creek rivermouth channel — the current runs swift and carries runoff, especially after rain; a paved path behind the lifeguard headquarters gets you around it instead. The cobblestone bottom hides sharp rocks and urchins at lower tide, and this is genuinely one of the least territorial breaks in Orange County — a real beginner and longboard spot, not a place to be nervous about paddling out.
