Break guide · Bali, Indonesia

Uluwatu

A reef break in Bali, Indonesia. Here is when it tends to be good, and what to know before you go.

When it works

Uluwatu picks up swell coming from the south-southeast round to the west. It's cleanest when the wind blows from the northeast, which is offshore here and smooths the waves.

The wave

A reef break. Waves break over reef or rock, so the bottom is shallow and hard.

Getting there and crowds

Access is a paddle out through the cave at Suluban Beach — at higher tides take real care coming back in or you can get swept past the cave toward Padang. Several sections spread along the cliff (Temples, The Peak, Racetracks, Outside Corner) hold everything from head high to well overhead, and Racetracks gets dangerously shallow at low tide. Dry season May–October brings dead-offshore SE trades and swell nearly every day; it is one of the most crowded world-class waves anywhere, so go at first light. Sharp reef — booties are smart.
