Noosa (First Point)

A point break in Queensland, Australia. Here is when it tends to be good, and what to know before you go.

When it works

Noosa (First Point) picks up swell coming from the north round to the east. It's cleanest when the wind blows from the south, which is offshore here and smooths the waves.

The wave

A point break. Waves wrap around a headland and peel along it.

Getting there and crowds

The longboard point — a slow, evenly peeling right off the Noosa National Park headland into Main Beach, with almost no sections when it lines up. It faces north, so it needs E-NE swell to break at all: cyclone season (roughly December-April) is when it happens, and much of the rest of the year it's flat. Low to mid tide with a southerly offshore gives the best shape. Gentle and beginner-friendly when small, and one of the most crowded lineups in Australia whenever it's on.
