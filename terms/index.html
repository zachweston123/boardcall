AI-written briefings

Briefings marked with a sparkle, and the weekly note Boardcall Pro writes from your logged sessions, are written by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic. For a briefing, Boardcall sends that model the day's conditions at your break, your skill level and weight, the boards it is choosing between, and your phone's language setting and chosen units (so the briefing comes back in your language and measurements). For a weekly note, it sends your skill level, a summary of your logged sessions (how many you've logged and, for each board and kind of conditions, the number of sessions and your median rating) and your three most recent session notes, word for word. Neither request includes your name, email, or location, but anything you type in a session note is sent as you wrote it. AI-written text can be wrong. Statements of fact in a briefing or note should not be relied on without checking them yourself, as they may be false, incomplete, misleading, or not reflect recent conditions. The board ranking itself is computed on your phone by a fixed set of rules; the AI writes the briefing and may explain a different choice from that ranked list.
