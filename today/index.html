The caption

Break of the Day: Black's Beach Lighting Up at Dawn

Black's is the steep beach break beneath the La Jolla cliffs in San Diego. At dawn, a mid-period groundswell out of the west is stacking up into shoulder to head high faces, with a glassy cross-shore breeze keeping the lines clean. Water's warm enough for a spring suit or trunks, no need for anything heavier. This is a day worth the drive.
