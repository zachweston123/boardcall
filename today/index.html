The caption

Break of the Day: Stinson Beach Wakes Up Well Overhead

Stinson Beach is a beach break tucked under the Marin County headlands north of San Francisco. At dawn it's lining up well overhead, pushed by a mid-period groundswell out of the WNW and groomed clean by a light offshore breeze. A 3/2 or spring suit covers the water temp fine, and the glass holds through the early morning before the day fills in. This is a day worth a drive.

Verdict: FIRE
