The caption

Break of the Day: Pico Alto Loads Up at Dawn

Pico Alto is a big-wave reef south of Lima, Peru, and today it's doing serious work. Faces are well overhead, pushed by a mid-period groundswell out of the southwest, with light cross-shore wind keeping the faces glassy. Dawn is the window to watch, before the day's wind pattern sets in. Water's warm enough for trunks. This is a day worth a drive.
